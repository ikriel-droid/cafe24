# Pricing And Packaging

## Packaging Hypothesis

Use simple operational packaging first.

### Starter
- one merchant
- low monthly claim volume
- inbox, dashboard, policy, Cafe24 integration
- manual reply handoff

### Growth
- higher claim volume
- live reply delivery integration
- diagnostics access
- onboarding support

### Plus
- advanced support expectations
- higher operator counts
- custom retention or reporting needs
- commercial review before activation

## Pricing Inputs

The first pricing model should consider:
- monthly claim volume
- number of active operators
- AI usage volume
- reply delivery volume
- support expectations

## Commercial Principles

- keep the first beta offer simple
- avoid charging on too many dimensions at once
- make overage rules obvious
- keep manual support expectations explicit
